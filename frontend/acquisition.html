The Acquisition Process

Our structured approach to property acquisition

Step 1: Registration & Profile

Register as a buyer and complete your investment profile. Tell us about your objectives, budget, preferences, and timeline.

Step 2: Opportunity Identification

Our team identifies relevant opportunities from our network and presents them to you with initial analysis and market context.

Step 3: Preliminary Review

We conduct initial analysis including location review, preliminary valuation, and financial modeling.

Step 4: Site Visit & Inspection

Organize site visits for you to view the property. We arrange professional inspections and condition assessments as needed.

Step 5: Due Diligence

Comprehensive due diligence including legal review, title verification, documentation checks, and professional assessments.

Step 6: Negotiation & Deal Structure

Support your negotiations with market data and advice on deal structuring and transaction terms.

Step 7: Transaction Completion

Coordinate with all parties to ensure smooth transaction completion, proper documentation, and fund transfer.
