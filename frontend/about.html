About MERD Landmark Properties

Connecting Capital With the Right Property Opportunities

Our Story

MERD Landmark Properties was founded with a clear mission: to simplify and professionalize real estate acquisitions for investors, businesses, and individuals in Nigeria.

We understand that finding the right property opportunity requires more than just listings. It requires market knowledge, research capabilities, professional networks, and a commitment to transparency.

Our team brings years of experience in real estate, investment, and business development. We've worked on residential acquisitions, commercial leases, industrial property transactions, and major development opportunities.

Our Mission

To empower property buyers and investors by providing curated opportunities, professional research, and transparent transaction support.

For Buyers

We help you find properties that match your objectives, understand the investment opportunity, and navigate the acquisition process with confidence.

For Sellers & Suppliers

We connect you with qualified, serious buyers and investors through our professional network and transparent process.
